Trust, Privacy & Terms

Transparent data protection standards, end-to-end recruiter privacy controls, and platform usage guidelines.

1. Information We Collect

NexusJobs collects information you provide directly during registration, profile updates, and direct role submissions. This includes:

  • Profile & Career Details: Full name, email address, portfolio URLs, GitHub handles, employment history, and uploaded PDF resumes.
  • Application Tracking: Submission logs, interview statuses, direct communication timestamps, and salary preferences.
  • Device & Analytics: IP address, browser type, device identifiers, and platform session activity.

2. How We Use Candidate Data

We process candidate information strictly to deliver seamless recruitment matches, verified hiring pipelines, and direct application routing. We will never sell your resume or personal data to third-party advertising brokers.

3. Recruiter Visibility & Profile Sharing

When you submit a 1-Click Application or enable public visibility in your settings, only verified enterprise recruiters can view your attached credentials. You can revoke access or toggle to private candidate mode at any time directly from the settings drawer.

1. Candidate Eligibility & Account Integrity

By accessing NexusJobs, you confirm that you are at least 18 years of age and that all experience credentials, skills, work histories, and uploaded portfolios accurately represent your individual work.

2. Authentic Compensation Transparency

All published job listings mandate verified salary ranges. Any employer found manipulating salary bands or engaging in bait-and-switch recruitment tactics will be permanently suspended from the Nexus platform.

3. Intellectual Property Rights

You retain complete ownership over all resumes, code snippets, project showcases, and design portfolios uploaded to your Nexus profile.

1. Encryption & Data Safeguards

All candidate data and resume documents are protected using TLS 1.3 encryption in transit and AES-256 encryption at rest within secure cloud environments.

2. Right to Data Deletion

You maintain full GDPR & DPDP compliance rights to download an archive of your profile activity or request permanent data deletion from Nexus servers at any time via the Help Center.
